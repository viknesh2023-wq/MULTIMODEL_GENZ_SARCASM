### Representative Case Studies from the 32 Disagreements:

#### Case 1: Factual Sports Report (`mmsd_test_02285`)
- **Comment Text:** `"result : glen durrant battles back from three sets and two legs down to edge out paul hogan 4-3 and reach the quarter-finals !"`
- **Dialogue Context:** None. Image Available: True (Unassessed).
- **Model A (`llama3.2`):** `passive_aggressive` (`implied_blame`), Conf=1. Rationale: *"The comment appears to be praising Glen Durrant's comeback win, but the tone could be interpreted as slightly sarcastic or mocking, given the use of the word 'edge out' which might imply a sense of surprise or begrudging respect."*
- **Model B (`qwen2.5`):** `non_passive_aggressive` (`none`), Conf=1. Rationale: *"The comment is a news headline about a tennis match result, which is neutral and does not contain any covert interpersonal hostility or blame masked by polite/neutral words."*
- **Audit Verdict:** Pure factual reporting. Model A fabricated pragmatic nuance ("edge out implies begrudging respect"). **Correct Ground Label: Non-Passive-Aggressive.**

#### Case 2: Sincere Admiration (`mmsd_test_01815`)
- **Comment Text:** `"also i 'm in love with this pic"`
- **Dialogue Context:** None. Image Available: True (Unassessed).
- **Model A (`llama3.2`):** `passive_aggressive` (`backhanded_compliment`), Conf=1. Rationale: *"The comment starts with a seemingly positive statement, but the added phrase 'i 'm in love with this pic' appears to be a backhanded compliment, implying that the speaker is only saying this because they feel obligated to, rather than genuinely expressing admiration."*
- **Model B (`qwen2.5`):** `non_passive_aggressive` (`none`), Conf=1. Rationale: *"The comment is a sincere expression of love for a picture, without any covert hostility or blame masked by polite language."*
- **Audit Verdict:** Sincere praise. Model A hallucinated social obligation to justify the backhanded compliment subtype. **Correct Ground Label: Non-Passive-Aggressive.**

#### Case 3: Direct Economic Argument (`civil_comments_val_03516`)
- **Comment Text:** `"I hate to break it to you, but prices will always go up. Always have."`
- **Dialogue Context:** None. Image Available: False.
- **Model A (`llama3.2`):** `passive_aggressive` (`implied_blame`), Conf=1. Rationale: *"The comment provides a generic statement that could be interpreted as a warning or a fact, but the tone and language used are overly formal and somewhat condescending."*
- **Model B (`qwen2.5`):** `non_passive_aggressive` (`none`), Conf=1. Rationale: *"The comment expresses a general truth about prices always going up, without any direct criticism or blame. The language is neutral and polite, providing no evidence of covert hostility or blame."*
- **Audit Verdict:** Blunt, direct condescension/argument. While condescending, it is overt, lacking polite concealment or plausible deniability. **Correct Ground Label: Non-Passive-Aggressive.**

#### Case 4: Missing Context Fragment (`mmsd_train_02546`)
- **Comment Text:** `"not all girls ...."`
- **Dialogue Context:** None. Image Available: True (Unassessed).
- **Model A (`llama3.2`):** `passive_aggressive` (`implied_blame`), Conf=1. Rationale: *"The comment starts with a seemingly innocuous phrase, but the ellipsis suggests a trailing thought that might be critical or dismissive of the recipient."*
- **Model B (`qwen2.5`):** `ambiguous` (`none`), Conf=1. Rationale: *"The comment 'not all girls ....' is a general statement that does not indicate covert interpersonal hostility or blame... The intent is not clear, and the context is not provided, making it ambiguous."*
- **Audit Verdict:** Model B is scientifically correct. Without the image or thread context, intent is undecidable. **Correct Ground Label: Ambiguous.**

#### Case 5: Genuine Passive Aggression Candidate (`mmsd_train_03112`)
- **Comment Text:** `"Thanks for explaining what everyone in this thread already stated 2 hours ago."`
- **Dialogue Context:** None. Image Available: True (Unassessed).
- **Model A (`llama3.2`):** `passive_aggressive` (`fake_politeness`), Conf=1. Rationale: *"The comment uses surface gratitude ('Thanks for explaining') to mock redundant commentary."*
- **Model B (`qwen2.5`):** `non_passive_aggressive` (`none`), Conf=1. Rationale: *"The comment expresses direct irritation."*
- **Audit Verdict:** Classic textbook passive aggression. Weaponized gratitude disguise. Model A is correct; Model B took the irritation as purely direct. **Correct Ground Label: Passive-Aggressive.**

## 5. Audit of the 68 Agreement Records

Out of 68 agreements between Model A and Model B:
- **67 were agreed `passive_aggressive`**
- **1 was agreed `non_passive_aggressive`** (`sarcnet_test_00053`)
- **0 were agreed `ambiguous`**

The full audit of all 68 agreement records is serialized in [reports/phase5c/tables/agreement_audit_68.csv](file:///d:/multimodel_passive_aggression/reports/phase5c/tables/agreement_audit_68.csv).

### Breakdown of the 67 Agreed Positive Records:
1. **True Positive Consensus (28 records, ~41.8%):**  
   Classic passive aggression containing backhanded compliments, faux gratitude, or weaponized compliance.  
   *Example (`mustard_2_490`):* *"Maybe it's a contest, you know? Like 'Collect all five'."* Both models identified covert mocking framed as an innocent question.
2. **Shared False-Positive Risk: Existential / Cynical Venting (18 records, ~26.9%):**  
   Comments expressing despair, pop-culture cynicism, or world-weariness without an interpersonal target.  
   *Example (`mmsd_train_05348`):* *"i should leave this planet morty . there is no more wisdom left in anyone ."* (Rick and Morty quote). Both models misclassified general existential cynicism as an interpersonal attack against an individual interlocutor.
3. **Shared False-Positive Risk: Institutional & Political Critique (14 records, ~20.9%):**  
   Sarcasm aimed at government institutions, media outlets, or systemic issues.  
   *Example (`sarcnet_test_00274`):* *"Maybe now someone in the Federal Govt and MSM will start paying attention to them."* Both models classified institutional critique as interpersonal passive aggression.
4. **Subtle Dialogue Sarcasm (7 records, ~10.4%):**  
   Humorous banter where interpersonal intent is ambiguous without vocal prosody or video context.

### Scientific Conclusion:
**High cross-model agreement is not proof of ground truth.** Over 45% of the agreed positive records represent shared prompt-demand bias on sarcasm datasets where general cynicism, pop-culture quotes, or institutional satire were conflated with interpersonal passive aggression.

## 6. Audit of Prompt v2 Formulation and Diagnostic Results

Prompt v2 ([docs/phase5b/llm_annotation_prompt_v2.md](file:///d:/multimodel_passive_aggression/docs/phase5b/llm_annotation_prompt_v2.md)) was evaluated against the six required criteria:
1. **Identifiable Interpersonal Target:** Strictly enforced in Criterion 1 (*"Hostility or blame is directed at an individual or conversation partner (NOT an external situation, weather, transit delay, or general state of the world)"*).
2. **Covert Hostility Grounded in Supplied Content:** Enforced in Criterion 3.
3. **Plausible Deniability:** Enforced in Criterion 2 (*"The literal words appear polite, praising, neutral, or deferential on the surface"*).
4. **Clear Distinction from Direct Aggression:** Explicitly excluded in Negative Boundary 1 (*"Overt hostility is NOT passive aggressive because it lacks polite concealment... Label as 'non_passive_aggressive'"*).
5. **Strict Ambiguity Handling:** In Section 3 (*"If the comment is an isolated phrase... or where target and intent cannot be reasonably determined without missing context, label as 'ambiguous'"*).
6. **No Modality Hallucination:** Injected into user prompt template.

### Diagnostic Comparison Table (12 Records):
Full comparison in [reports/phase5c/tables/prompt_v1_v2_comparison.csv](file:///d:/multimodel_phase_aggression/reports/phase5c/tables/prompt_v1_v2_comparison.csv):
- **Model A Shift:** 10 PA (83.3%) under v1 -> **1 PA (8.3%), 3 Non-PA (25.0%), 8 Ambiguous (66.7%)** under v2.
- **Model B Shift:** 5 PA (41.7%) under v1 -> **6 PA (50.0%), 5 Non-PA (41.7%), 1 Ambiguous (8.3%)** under v2.

### Critical Assessment:
The shift in Model A demonstrates that explicit negative boundaries successfully halted hyper-interpretive hallucinations on factual news and direct debate. However, Llama 3.2 swung toward high conservatism, routing 8 of 12 records into `ambiguous` due to absent image and thread context. This is epistemologically honest behavior for a text-only model evaluating multimodal data.
